Info

Since 2023 Michal has worked freelance. For film, she designed and animated digital interface simulations (spyware and live chat platforms), full motion graphic packages (title sequences, credits) and official posters for two Israeli short films. For music tours, cultural events and nightlife brands she makes dynamic posters, motion graphics and social media branding. For independent venues and art exhibitions she builds visual languages, logos, menus and exhibition catalogues. Her Bezalel capstone project was a 4.5-minute 3D animated short film, directed in Blender.
